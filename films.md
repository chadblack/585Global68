Provocative cinema from or about the era of 1968.

1. **2 or 3 Things I Know About Her** (1967). France. Dir. Jean-Luc Godard. Starring Marina Vlady, Anny Duperey, Joseph Gerhard.
2. **2001: A Space Oddessey** (1968). UK. Dir. Stanley Kubrick. Starring Keir Dullea, Gary Lockwood, William Sylvester, Daniel Richter.
3. **Ådalen 31** (1969). Sweden. Dir. Bo Widerberg.Starring Peter Schildt, Kerstin Tidelius, Roland Hedlund.
4. **All Monsters Attack** (1969). Japan. Ishiro Honda. 
5. **All My Good Countrymen** (1969). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Vojtech Jasný.
6. **The Alphabet** (1968). USA. Dir. David Lynch. Experimental short film of a sick woman's nightmare.   
7. **The American Soldier** (1970). West Germany. Dir. Rainer Werner Fassbinder. 
8. **L'amour fou** (1969). France. Jacques Rivette. Starring Bulle Ogier, Jean-Pierre Kalfon, André S. Labarthe
9. **Baldwin's N-----** (1968). UK. Dir. Horace Ové. Starring James Baldwin, Dick Gregory. Short documentary of Baldwin and Gregory speaking to West Indian students in London. 
10. **Barbarella** (1968). Dir. Roger Vadim. Starring Jane Fonda, John Phillip Law, Anita Pallenberg, Milo O'Shea.   
11. **Belle de jour** (1967). France. Luis Buñuel. Starring Catherine Deneuve, Jean Sorel, Michel Piccoli.
12. **Beyond the Law** (1968). USA. Norman Mailer. 
13. **The Big Shave** (1957). USA. Martin Scorsese. Short student film by Scorsese allegorizing the horrors of Vietnam.   
14. **Black Journal: Alice Coltrane** (1970). Dir. Stan Lathan. Short documentary film on the visionary musician.   
15. **Black Panthers** (1970). USA. Dir. Agnès Varda. Documentary on the Black Panther Party demonstrations demanding the release of Huey Newton.   
16. **The Blues Accordin' to Lightnin' Hopkins** (1968). USA. Les Blank. Early music documentary from one of the masters of the form.  
17. **A Boring Afternoon** (1968). Czechoslovakia. Ivan Passer.   
18. **The Boston Strangler** (1968). USA. Dir. Richard Fleischer. Starring Tony Curtis, Henry Fonda, George Kennedy.   
19. **La boutique** (1969). Spain. Luis García Berlanga. Starring Sonia Bruno, Rodolfo Bebán, Osvaldo Miranda.  
20. **Boy** (1969). Japan. Nagisa Oshima. Starring Tetsuo Abe, Fumio Watanabe, Akiko Koyam.
21. **Branded to Kill** (1967). Japan. Dir. Seijun Suzuki. Starring Joe Shishido, Koji Nanbara, Isao Tamagawa.  
22. **Bullitt** (1968). Dir. Peter Yates. Starring Steve McQueen, Jacqueline Bisset, Robert Vaughn, Don Gordon. 
23. **Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid** (1969). Dir. George Hill. Starring Paul Newman, Robert Redford, and Katherine Ross. 
24. **Butterfly** (1967). USA. Shirley Clark. Protest short film about Vietnam.  
25. **Calcutta** (1969). France. Dir. Louis Malle. Portrayal of a chaotic city in social and political turmoil.  
26. **Capricious Summer** (1968). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Jiři Menzel. 
27. **Chiefs** (1968). USA. Richard Leacock and Noel E. Parmental, Jr. Short film.  
28. **Coogan's Bluff** (1968). Dir. Don Siegel. Starring Clint Eastwood, Lee J. Cobb, Susan Clark, Tisha Sterling.   
29. **La collectionneuse** (1967). France. Dir. Eric Rohmer. Starring Haydée Politoff, Patrick Bauchau, Daniel Pommereulle.   
30. **The Color of Pomegranates** (1969). USSR. Dir. Sergei Parajanov.   
31. **A Colt is My Passport** (1967). Japan. Dir. Takashi Nomura. Starring Joe Shishido, Chitose Kobayashi. Japanese noir, yakuza film.   
32. **The Cremator** (1969). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Juraj Herz.  
33. **Crimes of the Future** (1970). Canada. Dir. David Cronenberg. Starring Ronald Mlodzik, Jon Lidolt, Tania Zolty. Bodies. Technology. You know... Cronenberg.   
34. **A Day with the Boys** (1969). USA. Dir. Clu Gulager. Short film in the imaginative world of a group of boys.  
35. **Death by Hanging** (1968). Japan. Dir. Nagisa Oshima. Starring Yung-do Yun, Kei Sato, Fumio Watanabe.   
36. **Destroy All Monsters** (1968). Japan. Dir. Ishiro Honda. 
37. **Diary of a Shinjuku Theif** (1969). Japan. Dir. Nagisa Oshima.  
38. **Dillinger is Dead** (1969). Italy. Dir. Marco Ferreri. Starring Michel Piccoli, Anita Pallenberg, Gino Lavagetto.   
39. **Double Suicide** (1969). Japan. Dir. Masahiro Shinoda. Starring Kichiemon Nakamura, Shima Iwashita, Hosei Komatsu.   
40. **Dragon Inn** (1967). Taiwan. Dir. King Hu. Innovative martial arts film.   
41. **The Ear** (1970). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Karel Kachyña. Surveillance thriller.   
42. **Elvira Madigan** (1967). Sweden. Dir Bo Widerberg. Starring Pia Degermark, Thommy Berggren, Lennart Malmer.   
43. **L'enfance nue** (1968). France. Dir. Maurice Pialat. Starring Michel Terrazon, Marie-Louise Thierry, René Thierry. Film about lost youth.   
44. **Faces** (1968). USA. Dir. John Cassavetes. Starring John Marley, Lynn Carlin, Gena Rowlands. Film about a failing marriage.   
45. **Fando y Lis**. Dir. Alejandro Jodorowsky. Starring Sergio Kleiner, Maria Teresa Rivas, Juan José Arreola, Tamara Garina. Jodorowsky's first feature-length film.
46. **Festival** (1967). USA. Murray Lerner. Documentary about the folk music scene around the Newport Folk Festival (1963-1966).   
47. **The Fireman's Ball** (1967). Czechoslovakia. Miloš Forman. Starring Jan Vostrcil, Josef Šebánek, František Reinstein. A definitive film of the Czech New Wave.   
48. **Flesh** (1968). Dir. Paul Morrissey. Starring Patti D'Arbanville. Produced by Andy Warhol.   
49. **Fruit of Paradise** (1970). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Věra Chytilová. Starring Jitka Nováková, Karel Novak, Jan Schmid. A retelling of Eden.   
50. **The Garden of Delights** (1970). Spain. Dir. Carlos Saura. A film about the psychic costs of living under a fascist regime.   
51. **Genocide** (1968). Japan. Dir. Kazui Nihonmatsu. Doomsday with insects.   
52. **Gimmie Shelter** (1970). USA. Dirs. David Maysles, Albert Maysles, and Charlotte Zwerin. The Rolling Stones's Tour in 1969, including Altamont.   
53. **The Girls** (1968). Sweden. Dir. Mai Zetterling. Starring Bibi Andersson, Harriet Andersson, Gunnel Lindblom. Feminist cinema.   
54. **God Respects Us When We Work, but Loves Us When We Dance** (1968). USA. Les Blank. Short documentary film on the LA love in in 1967.   
55. **Gods of the Plague** (1969). West Germany. Dir. Rainer Werner Fassbinder. Criminality, futility, romance.  
56. **Goke, Body Snatcher from Hell** (1968). Japan. Dir. Hajime Sato.  
57. **Golden Eyes** (1969). Japan. Dir. Jun Fukuda.  
58. **Le grand amour** (1969). France. Dir. Pierre Etaix. Satire of the bourgeoisie.    
59. **The Green Berets** (1968). Dirs: Ray Kellogg, John Wayne, Mervyn LeRoy. Starring John Wayne, David Janssen, Jim Hutton, Aldo Ray.   
60. **Honeycomb** (1969). Spain. Dir. Carlos Saura. Starring Geraldine Chaplin, Per Oscarsson, Emiliano Redondo. Trauma, regression, control, violence.   
61. **The Honeymoon Killers** (1969). USA. Dir. Leonard Kastle. Starring Shirley Stoler, Tony Lo Bianco. Desperate love and confidence men.   
62. **Hour of the Wolf** (1968). Sweden. Dir. Ingmar Bergman. Repressed desire and reality testing.   
63. **I Am Curious-Blue** and **I Am Curious-Yellow**(1967). Sweden. Dir. Vilgot Sjöman. A pair of films about Sweden's sexual revolutions.   
64. **The Immortal Story** (1968). France. Orson Welles. Starring Jeanne Moreau, Orson Welles, Roger Coggio. Welles's first color film, and last feature.   
65. **In the Folds of the Flesh** (1970). Italy. Dir. Sergio Bergonzelli. Starring Eleonora Rossi Drago, Fernando Sancho, Pier Angeli. Psychedelic thriller.   
66.  **In the Year of the Pig** (1968). Dir. Emile de Antonio. Documentary on the war in Vietnam.    
67. **Innocence Unprotected** (1968). Yugoslavia. Dir. Dušan Makavejev. Weird film.  
68. **Japanese Summer: Double Suicide** (1967). Japan. Dir. Nagisa Oshima. The death drive in Japanese youth.   
69. **The Joke** (1969). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Jaromil Jireš. Based on a Milan Kundera novel.   
70. **Katzelmacher** (1969). West Germany. Dir. Rainer Werner Fassbinder. Greek immigrants in West Germany.   
71. **Kill!** (1968). Japan. Dir. Kihachi Okamoto. Starring Tatsuya Nakadai, Etsushi Takahashi, Yuriko Hoshi. Action comedy about a pair of swordsmen.  
72. **Kuroneko** (1968). Japan. Dir. Kaneto Shindo. Starring Kichiemon Nakamura, Nobuko Otowa, Kei Sato. The monstrous feminine.   
73. **The Land** (1969). Egypt. Dir. Youssef Chahine. Starring Mahmoud el-Meliguy, Ezzat el-Alaili, Nagwa Ibrahim. The struggle of rural peasants in the 1930s.   
74. **El libro de piedra**. Dir. Carlos Enrique Taboada. Starring Marga López, Norma Lazareno. A Mexican horror film.   
75. **Lions Love (... and Lies)** (1969). USA. Dir. Agnès Varda. Starring Viva, Shirley Clarke, James Rado, Gerome Ragni. Counterculture metacinema.  
76. **The Living Skeleton** (1968). Japan. Dir. Hiroshi Matsuno. Revenge.   
77. **Meeting the Man: James Baldwin in Paris** (1970). UK. Dir. Terence Dixon.   
78. **Memories of Underdevelopment** (1968). Cuba. Dir. Tomás Gutiérrez Alea. Landmark film in Cuban cinema.   
79. **Monterey Pop** (1968). Dir. D.A. Pennebaker. Documentary on the 1967 Monterey Pop music festival.   
80. **Multiple Maniacs** (1970). USA. Dir. John Waters. Landing on the other side of the sexual revolution.  
81. **Night of the Living Dead**. (1968). USA. Dir. George Romero. Staring Duane Jones, Judith O'Dea, Karl Hardman, Marilyn Eastman.  
82. **The Odd Couple** (1968). USA. Dir. Gene Saks. Starring Jack Lemmon, Walter Matthau.   
83. **Once Upon a Time in the West** (1968). USA. Dir. Sergio Leone. Starring Henry Fonda, Charles Bronson, Claudia Cardinale, Jason Robards. 
84. **The Party** (1968). USA. Dir. Blake Edwards. Starring Peter Sellers, Claudine Longet, Natalia Borisova, Jean Carson.    
85. **La piscine** (1969). France. Dir. Jacques Deray. Starring Alain Delon, Romy Schneider, Maurice Ronet. 
86. **Planet of the Apes** (1968). USA. Dir. Franklin J. Schaffner. Starring Charlton Heston, Rody McDowall, Kim Hunter, Maurice Evans.   
87. **Playtime** (1967). France. Dir. Jacques Tati. Masterful satire of modernist Paris.   
88. **The Plot against Harry** (1969). USA. Dir. Michael Roemer. Starring Martin Priest, Ben Lang, Maxine Woods.   
89. **Porcile** (1969). Italy. Dir. Pier Paolo Pasolini. Starring Pierre Clémenti, Jean-Pierre Léaud, Alberto Lionello. “I killed my father. I ate human flesh. I quiver with joy.”   
90. **Portrait of Jason** (1967). USA. Dir. Shirley Clarke. Documentary of Hotel Chelsea resident "gay hustler and aspiring cabaret performer" Jason Holliday.   
91. **La prisonnière** (1968). France. Dir. Henri Georges-Clouzot. Starring Laurent Terzieff, Bernard Fresson, Elisabeth Wiener.  
92. **Profound Desire of the Gods** (1968). Japan. Dir. Shohei Imamura.  
93. **Return of the Prodigal Son** (1967). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Evald Schorm. Czech New Wave.   
94. **The Rite** (1969). Sweden. Ingmar Bergman. 
95. **Rome and Juliet** (1968). Dir. Fanco Zeffirelli. Starring Leonard Whiting, Olivia Hussey, John McEnery, Milo O'Shea.  
96. **Rosemary's Baby** (1968). Dir. Roman Polanski. Starring Mia Farrow, John Cassavetes, Ruth Gordon, Sidney Blackmer.   
97. **Salesman** (1969). USA. Dir. David Maysles, Albert Maysles, and Charlotte Zwerin. Direct cinema film about the corrosion of American dreams.   
98. **Saute ma ville** (1968). Belgium. Dir. Chantal Akerman. First film, a short film about the stifling domestic sphere and rebellion against it.   
99. **Le samourai** (1967). France, Italy. Starring Alain Delon, François Périer, Nathalie Delon. Contract killer.   
100. **Samurai Rebellion** (1967). Japan. Dir. Masaki Kobayashi. Starring Toshiro Mifune, Yoko Tsukasa, Go Kato.   
101. **Shame** (1968). Sweden. Dir. Ingmar Bergman. Starring Liv Ullmann, Max von Sydow.   
102. **The Shepherds of Calamity** (1967). Greece. Dir. Nico Papatakis. Made in the wake of the right wing coup in Greece in 1967.   
103. **Sing a Song of Sex** (1967). Japan. Dir. Nagisa Oshima. Students prepping for university entrance exams.   
104. **Soleil Ô** (1970). Mauritania. Dir. Med Hondo. Starring Robert Liensol, Théo Légitimus, Gabriel Glissand. Explosive attack on colonialism, western capitalism, etc.   
105. **Spider Baby** (1969). USA. Dir. Jack Hill. Starring Lon Chaney Jr, Carol Ohmart, Jill Banner. Cult classic horror schlock.   
106. **Spirits of the Dead** (1968). Dirs. Federico Fellini, Louis Malle, Roger Vadim. Starring Jane Fonda, Brigitte Bardot, Alain Delon, Terence Stamp.    
107. **Stereo** (1969). Canada. Dir. David Cronenberg. His first feature, featuring already themes he likes such as medical experiments and telepathy.   
108. **Stolen Kisses** (1968). France. Dir. François Truffaut. Starring Jean-Pierre Léaud, Delphine Seyrig, Michael Lonsdale.  
109. **The Story of a Three Day Pass** (1967). France. Dir. Melvin Van Peebles. Starring Harry Baird, Nicole Berger, Hal Brav.   
110. **Stress is Three** (1968). Spain. Dir. Carlos Saura. Starring Geraldine Chaplin, Juan Luis Galiardo, Fernando Cebrián.   
111. **The Student Nurses** (1970). USA. Dir. Stephanie Rothman. Feminist film critiquing the cultural world entering the new decade.   
112. **The Swimmer** (1968). Dir. Frank Perry, Syndey Pollack. Starring Burt Lancaster, Janet Landgard, Janice Rule, Tony Bickley.  
113. **Teorema** (1968). Italy. Dir. Pier Paolo Pasolini. Starring Terence Stamp, Silvana Mangano, Massimo Girotti. Film about the spiritual emptiness of the bourgeoisie.  
114. **Thirst for Love** (1967). Japan. Dir. Koreyoshi Kurahara. A tale of sexual betrayal and family obsession.   
115. **Three Resurrected Drunkards** (1968). Japan. Dir. Nagisa Oshima. Indictment of Japanese treatment of Koreans.   
116. **Toby dammit** (1968). Italy. Dir. Federico Fellini. Starring Terence Stamp. Based on a Poe story. Part of **Spirits of the Dead**.   
117. **Topaz** (1969). USA. Alfred Hitchcock. Starring Federick Stafford, Dany Robin, John Vernon. Cold War thriller.   
118. **The Unfortunate Bridegroom** (1967). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Jiří Krejčík. Starring Iva Janžurová, Vladimír Pucholt, Pavel Landovský.   
119. **Valerie and Her Week of Wonders** (1970). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Jaromil Jireš. 
120. **The Valley of the Bees** (1968). Czechoslovakia. Dir. František Vláčil. Starring Petr Čepek, Jan Kačer, Věra Galatíková.   
121. **The Virgin of Pessac** (1969). France. Dir. Jean Eustache. A documentary film about tradition in the village of Pessac.  
122. **Viy** (1967). USSR. Dirs. Konstantin Yershov and Georgi Kropachyov. Starring Leonid Kuravlyov, Natalya Varley, Vadim Zakharchenko. An undead witch in 19th-c. Ukraine and the seminary student who must spend three nights with her corpse.   
123. **Weekend** (1967). France. Dir. Jean-Luc Godard. Satirizing the French bourgeoisie.   
124. **Wild 90** (1968). USA. Dir. Norman Mailer. His first feature film.   
125. **Witchfinder General** (1968). Dir. Michael Reeves. Starring Vincent Price, Ian Ogilvy, Rupert Davies, Hilary Heath.  
126. **Witchhammer** (1970). Czechoslovakia. Dir. Otakar Vávra. Starring Vladimír Šmeral, Elo Romančík, Josef Kemr.   
127. **Yellow Submarine** (1968). Dir. George Dunning. Starring Paul McCartney, George Harrison, Ringo Starr, John Lennon.   
128. **Z** (1969). Algeria, France. Dir. Costa-Gavras. Starring Yves Montand, Jean-Louis Trintignant, Irene Papas. Political thriller.   
129. **Zuckerkandl!** (1968). USA. Dir John Hubley. Animated short film parody of Sigmund Freud.
